How the Majdur Therapy Zone Works

Need a tiny reset during a long workday? Pick a game and take a short mental break. Use the Break Decider when nobody can decide whether it is chai time, use the Colleague Game to settle a harmless office challenge, or spin the wheel when you want a random truth-or-dare style task. These are entertainment features created for the Corporate Majdur community.

☕ Break Decider

Ask the Corporate Oracle for a funny break suggestion, then get back to the grind.

👥 Colleague Game

Choose a cup one by one. One unlucky cup becomes the bill payer.

⏱️ Escape Timer

Set a short break timer so your chai break has a clear beginning and end.

🌀 Fate Wheel

Spin for a safe result or a playful truth-or-dare challenge with your group.

A note for employees: These games are meant for entertainment and short breaks. Keep your workplace policies, responsibilities and break timings in mind.
